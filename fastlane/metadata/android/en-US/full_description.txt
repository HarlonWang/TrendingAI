Trending AI is a curated feed of open-source tech trends, built for developers. It aggregates three of the most popular tech sources — GitHub Trending, Hacker News, and Product Hunt — then uses AI to pick the standout stories and explain them, so you can keep up with global tech trends in the least time possible.

[Daily Picks — AI Curated]
Every day we hand-pick the most noteworthy items from all three sources, each paired with an AI-generated summary and in-depth analysis. Catch up on the day's tech highlights in 5 minutes and leave information overload behind.

[Key Features]
- AI Summaries — Every project comes with an AI-generated breakdown so you can grasp its value at a glance
- History Browsing — Revisit Trending data from any date and never miss a hot topic
- Local Bookmarks — Save anything that catches your eye with one tap and read it later

[Privacy & Transparency]
The app provides a full in-app privacy policy, stores data locally, and ships with zero ad SDKs. We stay transparent and restrained when it comes to your data.

[Feedback & Support]
An in-app feedback form lets you send opinions, suggestions, or issues anytime. The source code is hosted on GitHub — Stars, Issues, and PRs are all welcome.

Built for developers, engineers, tech leaders, founders, indie hackers, and anyone keeping an eye on global tech trends.
